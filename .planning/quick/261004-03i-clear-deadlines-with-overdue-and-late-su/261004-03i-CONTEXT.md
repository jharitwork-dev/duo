### Deadline semantics (pure, unit-tested helper `src/lib/deadline.ts`)
- Effective deadline of a to-do = `todo.deadline ?? phase.deadline ?? null`. All formatting and comparisons use Asia/Bangkok (reuse
  `src/lib/format.ts` helpers; add `formatDeadline` → "ส่งภายใน 18 ต.ค. 23.59" and relative "อีก 2 วัน" / "เหลือ 5 ชม." / "เลยกำหนด 1 วัน").
- Deadline status of a to-do for a group/student (`getDeadlineStatus({ deadline, firstSubmittedAt, latestStatus, now })`):
  - `none`: no deadline.
  - `upcoming`: not submitted, more than 48 h left.
  - `due_soon`: not submitted, ≤ 48 h left (yellow).
  - `overdue` **"เลยกำหนด"**: not submitted and the deadline has passed (red/orange).
  - `on_time` **"ส่งตรงเวลา"**: first submission ≤ deadline (green).
  - `late` **"ส่งช้า"** with how late ("ช้า 2 วัน"): first submission > deadline (orange).
  - Lateness is judged on the FIRST submission. A resubmission after "ต้องแก้ไข" does not change on_time→late. A rejected item past its
    deadline with no new submission shows its review status plus the deadline badge (it was already submitted once). Note this rule in the
    UI tooltip/help text.
- The deadline status is separate from, and shown alongside, the review status (ยังไม่ส่ง / รอตรวจ / ต้องแก้ไข / ผ่านแล้ว).

### Where it shows (Cocoon CI)

### Edit after submitting, before the deadline (user decisions 2026-10-04)
"ส่งแล้วอยากให้แก้ไขได้ก่อน deadline"
- While the latest submission is `pending` (not yet reviewed) and `now < effective deadline`, the work page stays **editable**. The submit bar
  shows **"อัปเดตงานที่ส่ง"**, which re-snapshots the page and its files INTO THE SAME pending submission (still "ส่งครั้งที่ n"; store
  `submissions.updated_at` and show "อัปเดตล่าสุด …" to student and teacher). There is no new round and no lateness change.
- **No deadline:** editable/updatable until the teacher reviews it (approved or rejected).
- After the deadline passes, a pending submission is locked (read-only, "เลยกำหนดแก้ไขแล้ว"). Students can still post comments.
- `approved` is always locked. `rejected` works as today: edit, then "ส่งอีกครั้ง" creates round n+1.
- Server-side enforcement in `work-page.ts` (`canEditWorkPage`/`canSubmitWorkPage` become deadline-aware via `getEffectiveDeadline`), with a
  unit-test matrix: status × (no deadline / before / after) × action (edit / update / submit).
- Conflict safety: if a teacher starts or finishes a review between the student's load and their update, the update is rejected with
  "ครูตรวจงานนี้แล้ว" and the page refreshes.
- Teacher views show the latest snapshot plus "อัปเดตเมื่อ …"; history keeps only the final snapshot per round (no per-update history).
- No new tables. `submissions.updated_at` already exists. Make sure it is set on update.

### Data
No schema change needed: `todos.deadline` and `phases.deadline` exist, and `submissions.created_at` gives the first submission time. Do NOT add columns.

Out of scope: notifications/reminders (Phase 6 LINE), grading penalties, editing a deadline per group (one deadline per to-do row; multi-group
copies are separate rows so they can differ).
